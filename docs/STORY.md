# The story

The film follows Moses' life in short chapters, each a few minutes long, each
built around a handful of touches. Scripture references are the source for
every beat; the film stays close to the text and leaves its words unspoken —
the voice of God is sound and light, never subtitles.

Threads worth carrying through (one possible reading): the staff, the
lamb's blood at Passover and the lifted serpent are images the New Testament
picks up (John 1:29, John 3:14–15, 1 Corinthians 5:7), so their visual
language could rhyme across chapters. Hebrews 11:23–29 summarises Moses' life
as one of faith and could frame the opening and ending.

## Chapter 1 — The Basket (built)

1. Goshen, at night. A mother hums to her newborn son.
2. Soldiers in the street (1:22). *(touch the lamp)* She puts it out and
   hides him; they stop at the window, then pass.
3. "She hid him three months": the moon crosses the window three times.
4. He can no longer be hidden. *(touch ×3)* she weaves the ark of bulrushes;
   *(touch)* daubs it with pitch; lays him inside; *(touch)* closes the lid.
5. Dawn at the Nile. *(touch the basket)* She lets it go among the flags;
   she goes, and his sister stays (2:4). *(touch Miriam)* she follows it.
6. Day. Pharaoh's daughter comes down to wash (2:5). *(touch the basket)* her
   maid draws it out.
7. *(touch the lid)* "Behold, the babe wept." *(touch the child)* "And she had
   compassion on him": her hand, and his hand closing round her finger.
8. *(touch Miriam)* "Shall I go and call thee a nurse?" She runs for their
   mother, and the princess gives the child back to his own mother (2:9).
   "I drew him out of the water" (2:10).

## Chapter 4 — The Burning Bush (built)

1. Dusk on the plain. A light on the mountain. *(touch the light, or Moses)*
2. He turns aside to see. Night; Horeb; the bush burns and is not consumed.
   *(touch the fire)*
3. "Moses, Moses" — two calls in the fire; he falls on his face.
4. Holy ground. *(touch each sandal to take it off)*
5. He hides his face. The cry of his people: a vision of slaves and a whip in the flames.
6. "I will send thee" — a path of light back toward Egypt; he hesitates.
7. The signs. *(touch the staff)* it becomes a serpent; he flees, then
   *(touch the serpent)* takes it by the tail. *(touch the hand ×4)* into the
   cloak, out leprous, in again, restored.
8. Dawn. *(touch him or the path)* He goes.
